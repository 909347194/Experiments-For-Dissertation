An LLM acts as a plug-in controller for differential evolution.

Population seeding and crossover rate are decided by the language model.

The model receives feedback on its previous decision, closing the control loop.

Evolutionary operators stay untouched, so the method is easy to adopt.

LLM seeding helps only when its candidates can be repaired to feasibility.
