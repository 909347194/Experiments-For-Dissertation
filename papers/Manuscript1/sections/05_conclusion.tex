\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented an LLM-enhanced differential evolution framework for
multi-UAV cooperative path planning in which a pretrained language model acts as
a plug-in controller for population initialization and crossover-rate
adaptation, leaving the evolutionary operators unchanged. The framework was
evaluated through an ablation study on a balanced and a scarce-resource scenario.

The study also exposes a limitation that we consider practically important: the
benefit of LLM-driven seeding depends on whether the generated candidates can be
repaired to feasibility, which does not hold in tightly constrained scenarios.
Future work will therefore target constraint-aware prompting and a repair
mechanism that is informed by the constraint structure rather than by
